# FIFA World Cup 2022 - Argentina Performance Analysis

**University of Sydney | Grade: High Distinction (14/15)**

## Overview

Data analysis of Argentina's defensive and offensive performance across their 2022 FIFA World Cup campaign. The project examines how Argentina's presence affected opponent behaviour by comparing opponent statistics in matches against Argentina vs their other tournament games.

## Research Focus

How did Argentina's performance suppress opponent attacking output across the group stage and knockout rounds?

## Dataset

- 64 total matches in the 2022 FIFA World Cup
- 30 matches filtered: all games involving Argentina's opponents, including their matches against Argentina
- 6 key variables: possession, goals scored, goals conceded, total shots, shots inside the penalty box, shots outside the penalty box

## Key Findings

- Argentina's opponents recorded significantly lower possession when facing Argentina compared to their other matches
- Opponents were consistently forced into a higher proportion of shots from outside the penalty box, reflecting Argentina's defensive strength in restricting easier chances
- Group stage opponents (Mexico, Saudi Arabia, Poland) generated more than double the shots in matches not involving Argentina
- High-calibre knockout opponents (France, Croatia, Netherlands) also struggled to generate shot volume against Argentina
